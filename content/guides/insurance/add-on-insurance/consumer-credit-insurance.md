Consumer credit insurance (CCI) is sold with credit cards, personal loans, and car finance. It promises to help with repayments if you die, become disabled, or sometimes lose your job. The pitch is convenience at the point of sale. The problem is cost, narrow definitions, and waiting periods that leave many claims unpaid.

## What CCI usually covers—and what it skips

Policies vary, but benefits often include:

- a lump sum toward the loan on death
- repayments for a limited time on disability
- a capped period of repayments after involuntary unemployment

Exclusions are where value dies. Pre-existing health conditions, casual or contract work that fails the unemployment definition, resignation, misconduct dismissals, and probation periods are frequent blockers. Disability definitions may demand total inability to work, not “reduced hours.” Mental health limitations appear in some wordings.

Waiting periods apply after the policy starts and again after a job-loss or disability event before benefits accrue. If your emergency fund cannot bridge those waits, CCI will not save the first missed payments anyway.

## How the price sneaks into the loan

CCI premiums are often capitalised into the loan balance. You then pay interest on the insurance. A “small monthly amount” can become a large total cost over the loan term—sometimes rivalling or exceeding the protection you would get from a modest term life or income protection policy held separately.

Ask for the total premium, whether it is financed, the claim caps (often only 12 months of repayments), and the cooling-off period. Compare that total with the cost of increasing [life cover](/topics/insurance/life-insurance/life-insurance-cover) or checking [income protection](/topics/insurance/life-insurance/income-protection-insurance) you may already hold in super.

Credit card CCI charged as a percentage of the closing balance can punish people who carry debt for years. If you pay the card off monthly, you may be buying little useful risk cover at a high relative price.

## Better questions before you tick the box

- Do I already have life, TPD, or income protection that would service this debt?
- Is the unemployment benefit limited to permanent employees with a minimum tenure?
- What waiting periods apply, and can I fund them?
- Can I buy the loan without CCI, and will saying no change the interest rate? (It should not be a condition of approval for the credit itself in normal retail lending—pressure is still common.)
- What is the cancellation refund if I exit in month two?

If a lender implies you cannot get the loan without CCI, pause and get the refusal in writing. Shop another lender if needed.

## If you already bought CCI

Use cooling-off if you are still inside it. Outside cooling-off, ask for a cancellation quote and whether a pro-rata refund applies. Redirect the freed cash flow to an emergency fund or to debt reduction—the same risks CCI marketed, funded more flexibly.

Keep the PDS in case you do claim: document job loss or medical evidence early, and diary waiting-period end dates.


## A worked cost check before you keep paying

Open the loan contract and find the CCI premium. If it was financed, ask the lender for the interest attributable to that premium over the full term. Add those figures. Then ask what the maximum disability or unemployment benefit period is—often months, not years.

Compare that total cost with one year of premiums on a simple term life policy sized to the loan balance, or with increasing your existing income protection slightly. If CCI wins on price and definitions after that comparison, keep it. If not, cancel and redirect the repayment difference into an offset or emergency account labelled for the same risks.

## What to do next

- Pull the CCI schedule on any active loan or card and total the premiums over the full term.
- Map benefits against cover you already hold in super or retail policies.
- Cancel within cooling-off if you did not intend to buy it; otherwise request cancellation terms in writing.
- Build a cash buffer sized to the waiting periods you would face without CCI.
- For genuine income or death risk, compare proper [income protection](/topics/insurance/life-insurance/income-protection-insurance) and [life insurance](/topics/insurance/life-insurance/life-insurance-cover) rather than loan-desk add-ons.
