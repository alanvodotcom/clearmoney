## How to think about the moving parts

Setup quality decides whether **super contributions** helps or nags you later. Confirm identity and contact details, choose alerts you will actually read, and decide who else can see or move money. If a salesperson is present, ask for the key numbers in writing before you sign.

People often skip the boring middle: documents, dates, and who else is affected. Write those down before you compare shiny features. If two options look identical, the tie-breaker is usually fees, exit flexibility, or how painful a mistake would be to reverse.

### Easy-to-miss costs

### Recovery friction

Missing fine print on fees or cooling-off rights. Keep PDFs and screenshots; verbal promises are hard to enforce later.
