Retirement planning works better when people can share the same short resources—with family, in a workplace session, or in a community group. This page collects ClearMoney’s retirement campaign-style materials in one place.

## Who these resources are for

- People approaching retirement who want a simple starting checklist
- Partners comparing “what if we stop work at different ages?”
- Community educators looking for plain-language stories (not product pitches)
- First Nations readers looking for culturally aware pathways and services

Everything here is general information only—not personal financial advice.

## Core planning pack

Start with the [Retirement checklist](/topics/super-retirement/retirement/retirement-checklist). It covers spending needs, debts, super access timing, and Age Pension questions in one pass.

Then run the numbers:

- [Retirement planner](/tools/retirement-planner) — nest-egg need vs projected super
- [Superannuation calculator](/tools/super) — balance growth with contributions and fees
- [Super and pension age calculator](/tools/super-pension-age) — when you can access super and Age Pension age

## Case studies to discuss

Stories help people see trade-offs without needing their own spreadsheet first:

- [Helen and Joe retire](/topics/super-retirement/retirement/case-study-helen-and-joe-retire) — blending super drawdowns and Age Pension
- [Bill’s mortgage decision](/topics/super-retirement/retirement/case-study-bills-mortgage-decision) — paying off the home before or during retirement
- [Lillian’s health scare](/topics/super-retirement/retirement/case-study-lillians-health-scare) — how health costs change the plan

Use them as conversation starters: “Which part of this story feels closest to us?”

## How to use this pack in a workshop

1. Open with the checklist—everyone writes one retirement goal in a sentence.
2. Pick one case study and discuss what they would do differently.
3. Point people to the calculators; they can try numbers privately on their phone.
4. Close with [Urgent help](/urgent) contacts if anyone is in debt stress or scam risk now.

## What to do next

- Complete the [retirement checklist](/topics/super-retirement/retirement/retirement-checklist).
- Try the [retirement planner](/tools/retirement-planner) with a few different retirement ages.
- Read one case study with your partner or a trusted friend.
- If cold callers push a “free super check,” hang up—see [Protect your super from pushy sales calls](/topics/super-retirement/grow-super/protect-your-super-from-pushy-sales-calls).
