Unclaimed money programs will not pay on a hunch. They need enough evidence to show you are the person (or authorised representative) entitled to the funds. Building that pack before you lodge saves months of back-and-forth.

## What claimants are asked to prove

Most schemes test three ideas:

1. **You are who you say you are** — identity documents that meet their list
2. **You are connected to the money** — the account, policy, holding, wage, or company interest matches your history
3. **You have authority to receive it** — especially if the original owner has died or the money belonged to a company or trust

Fail any one of those and the claim stalls. “I definitely remember that account” without documents is not enough.

Certified copies are often preferred over originals sent by post. Follow the scheme’s certification rules (Justice of the Peace, police station, or listed occupations).

## Identity, connection, and authority

**Identity.** Passport, driver licence, proof of age card, and secondary documents as required. If your current name differs from the name on the unclaimed record, include change-of-name or marriage certificates.

**Connection.** Old bank statements, shareholding statements, policy schedules, payslips, letters addressed to the dormant account holder, or tax records that mention the asset. Even a single page with the account number and your name can unlock the rest of the file.

**Authority.** Enduring power of attorney (while the person is alive and the document is valid for this purpose), probate, letters of administration, or company appointment documents. Agencies are strict here because paying the wrong person creates liability.

Map your documents against those three columns on one page before you upload anything.

## When you claim for someone else

Common scenarios:

- **Executor or administrator** — estate money; probate documents usually required once thresholds kick in
- **Beneficiary of a life policy** — policy schedule naming you, plus death certificate
- **Parent or guardian** — for a child’s amount, proof of parental responsibility as the scheme defines it
- **Attorney** — valid power of attorney covering financial matters

If several siblings could claim, decide who lodges and ensure others will not lodge competing applications with conflicting stories. Agencies freeze progress when claims collide.

For company-related money, authority may mean showing you are a creditor, shareholder, or liquidator with standing—see [claim money owed to a deregistered company](/topics/community/unclaimed-money/claim-money-owed-to-a-deregistered-company).

## Building a paper trail from scraps

Many successful claims start with fragments:

- A faded dividend advice
- An old address on a letter
- A tax return dividend schedule
- A relative’s memory of the bank branch suburb

Use fragments to request better records: ask the bank or registry for historical statements once you can prove identity. Search email for PDFs of statements. Check storage boxes for passbooks.

Create a simple index: document name, date, what it proves (identity / connection / authority). Upload in that order. Label files clearly (`licence-jane-smith.pdf`, not `scan42.pdf`).

Protect your privacy—use official portals, not third-party sites that ask for full ID packages up front for a “free search.”

## Translation and overseas documents

If key documents are in another language or issued overseas, ask the scheme whether certified translations are required. Start that process early—it adds weeks. Community organisations sometimes help with translation referrals for official purposes.

## What to do next

- Draw three headings—identity, connection, authority—and list documents under each.
- Obtain certified copies that meet the scheme’s rules before you lodge.
- For estates and policies, confirm whether probate or beneficiary proof is required.
- Lodge through the official channel for that register ([banks](/topics/community/unclaimed-money/claim-money-from-bank-accounts-and-bank-dividends), [shares](/topics/community/unclaimed-money/claim-money-from-shares-and-investments), [life insurance](/topics/community/unclaimed-money/claim-money-from-life-insurance-policies), or [states](/topics/community/unclaimed-money/money-held-by-state-governments)).
- Keep a claim reference and a folder of everything submitted.
